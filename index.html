Overview

Supporting independent mobility through shared autonomy.

CyFI explores how semiautonomous cycling can support blind and low-vision riders while keeping the rider actively engaged in propulsion, braking, and interaction with the surrounding environment.

CyFI

CyFI is a semiautonomous, human-powered recumbent tricycle with onboard sensing and computation, actuated steering and braking, and multimodal rider feedback. The system can autonomously steer and initiate safety braking while the rider provides propulsion and retains manual braking control. Audio and vibrotactile feedback communicate system behavior and environmental information, supporting evaluation across progressively more realistic cycling environments.
